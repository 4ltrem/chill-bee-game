## Changes

1. **Welcome screen.** On every launch an overlay (CanvasLayer in `main.tscn`, not a separate scene) shows over the live farm, dimmed: the title "Chill Bee Game" and one **Play** button, nothing else (no how-to text, no icons). Until Play the world is idle: no hive fill, bees hold, no input reaches the farm. Play removes the overlay and shows the farm UI. Config `show_welcome` (bool, default true); every test run sets it false unless the test is about the welcome screen. The title is not translated; "Play" goes through `tr()` (key `welcome_play`, en "Play", fr "Jouer").

2. **Habitat Health.** `Economy` holds `habitat_health`, starting at `habitat_health_start` (10). Nothing lowers it in 0.3. It is capped at `habitat_health_max` (100). When it reaches `habitat_thriving` (100), `Economy` emits `thriving` once per farm. Health shows as a count pill in the farm view's top info, in `UI_DESIGN.md` rule 9 order: the number, a wildflower icon (not a leaf: rule 6 keeps the leaf for Impact), then **+**, which opens the Shop. Farm view only, like the rest of the info row. The pill sits beside the money in one row at the top of `TopInfo` (designer, 2026-11-22: "beside the money"), with the bee count below, so its 120 px + button (V11) does not make the info stack overlap the hive count. The pill clears the Harvest, Spin and Sell anchors, the hive count and the skip icon.

3. **Plant wildflower patch.** A new Shop row in its own Tree C group under the Tree A/B rows: "Plant wildflower patch", level "n / 6", cost `patch_base_cost × patch_cost_growth^planted` ($40 × 1.25^n, floored; $449 for all six), available from the start (no gate). A purchase shows the next of `patch_slots` (6) hidden patches placed in `main.tscn` (instances of `scenes/props/flower_patch.tscn`, children of a `PatchSlots` node using `patch_slots.gd`, planted in child order; a hidden slot is not in group `flower_patch` until planted, so at start the group still holds only the three decorative patches), with a grow-in (scale 0 → 1, the existing pop tween family), adds it to group `flower_patch` so the bees forage on it, adds `habitat_health_per_patch` (15) to Health, and shows the toast `toast_patch_planted`. At 6 / 6 the row shows "Thriving" in place of a price. Patches raise habitat only: no honey effect. The slots sit clear of both camera stops' framing and of the truck's path. With `shop_layout = 0` (compact panel) the row is the panel's eighth row and all eight rows still show at 720 × 1280 without scrolling (0.2 item 14). The open Shop (panel or sheet) may cover the Harvest, Sell and Spin icons (designer, 2026-11-22: "the shop screen can overlap any harvest, sell or spin icons as it takes most of the screen"), so the panel grows taller for the eighth row rather than shrinking rows or scrolling; this replaces 0.2 item 14's "still clears the HUD". With 120 px buttons (V11) the eight rows and the Journal button no longer fit under the top info, so the open Shop is a full-height sheet over the whole screen, top info included (designer, 2026-11-22: "Shop covers the whole screen"); its Close button and a tap outside the rows return to the farm. Its rows must not overlap each other, and every row and the Journal button stay on screen at 720 × 1280.

4. **The bumble bee visitor.** On `thriving`, the toast `toast_bumble_visitor` shows and the Bee Journal (the 0.2 Bumble Bees entry, item 8) unlocks. Before thriving the Journal button is hidden; from thriving on it shows and opens the entry as in 0.2; the Dev "Reset habitat" hides it again. No bee model.

5. **Victory screen.** After `thriving`, the victory is queued: it shows the next time the player is on the farm view with no zoom, sheet or drag in progress, after `victory_delay_seconds` (4.0). It never interrupts a harvest. An overlay over the live farm, dimmed, with the new patches visible behind it: `victory_title`, `victory_body`, and one button, `victory_button` ("Keep playing"), which closes it. No summary, timer or record. It shows once per farm. After it, play continues unchanged (honey, upgrades, truck), the pill stays at 100 and the row stays maxed. Strings through `tr()` from the approved `content/course-ending-text.md` rows, exported to a translation CSV by script, not retyped.

6. **Skip to ending.** A small skip icon at the farm view's edge (designer, 2026-11-22: visible for graders, not on the welcome screen), with the label `skip_icon_label` as its tooltip/accessibility text. Pressing it plants every remaining patch for free (each with its grow-in), so Health reaches thriving and items 4 and 5 follow as in normal play. It hides once thriving is reached, and it is not shown (or pressable) while the welcome screen is up. It is a `SkipIcon` Button (`skip_icon.gd`) in the farm UI of `main.tscn`. Clears every other farm-view control.

## Dev mode and config

All values in `default_config.tres` and live in the Dev panel: `show_welcome`, `habitat_health_start`, `habitat_health_per_patch`, `habitat_thriving`, `habitat_health_max`, `patch_slots`, `patch_base_cost`, `patch_cost_growth`, `victory_delay_seconds`. Dev buttons: "Show welcome", "Show victory", "+1 patch (free)", "Health to thriving − 1 patch", "Reset habitat" (Health to start, patches hidden, victory and visitor flags cleared, Journal hidden again), "Unlock Journal". `victory_target_minutes` (25) is Terry's play-test target, not a game value.
