# 0.1.2 Bottling and sale

**Builds on** `game/specs/0.1-harvest.md` and `game/specs/0.1.1-playtest-fixes.md` (both approved; 0.1.1 is built on `milestone/0.1` but not yet tested or exported, so this build tests and exports both). Only the changes below differ. Process: `game/CONTEXT.md`.

## Changes

1. **Zoom option A** (designer's pick, merged in `ee1da9a`): the zoom frames the **whole risen rack** with every cell ≥ 44 px; the bucket shows at least its rim and mouth (where cells land); its lower part may be off-screen. This replaces 0.1.1's "hive, risen frame and bucket all in view".

2. **Hive about half the truck.** The truck is about 3.2 long. The hive becomes a small backyard box beside it: footprint about 1.6 × 1.6 or less, and its height with the lid on about half the truck's roof height. It stays square to the grid with frames facing +X (0.1.1). Frames and cells scale with it; the zoom (option A) tightens so cells stay ≥ 44 px. 0.1.1's "footprint = the bucket's" is retired.

3. **The centrifuge lid lands clear.** Its open pose (`CentrifugeLidSpot`) moves further from the bucket, so the resting lid touches neither the bucket, its base ring, the spigot, the hive nor the crate. The lift-then-fall path stays.

4. **Colour and glow are not checked** (designer: "don't worry about the honey glow or color"). The capped and uncapped colour measurements become report-only (numbers in the report, no pass/fail). The pop snapshot keeps its projected-size comparison; its pixel-area half is dropped.

5. **Jars come from the crate.** The crate shows **empty** jars waiting (clear glass, no honey, no lid), up to `crate_empty_jars_shown` (10). The supply is unlimited in this prototype (a jar taken is replaced in the crate).
   - When bottling starts, the first empty jar **flies** from the crate to under the spigot (`jar_fly_seconds`), fills over `bottling_seconds_per_jar` (honey rising, as 0.1.1), gets its lid, then flies to the truck's bed, or **back into the crate, full with its lid**, while the truck is away. Then the next empty jar flies in. Jars in flight and at the spigot are visual; the count logic is unchanged (a jar is bottled when its fill time ends).
   - The crate shows full jars apart from the empties (e.g. full jars on one side, empties on the other); when the truck returns, the full ones fly onto it up to its capacity.

6. **No Continue.** When extraction is done, the spin winds down and bottling **starts by itself**. `ContinueButton` and the `DONE` wait are removed (the state may still pass through `DONE` for one frame if the code needs it).

7. **The batch summary moves to after Sell.** Bottling no longer shows a summary. Tapping **Sell** credits the money and sends the truck off as now, and the summary modal shows **jars sold, price per jar, total** (no tool or quality bonus lines), with **OK** to close. The truck drives off behind it.
