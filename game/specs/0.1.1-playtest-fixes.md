# 0.1.1 Play-test fixes

**Builds on** `game/specs/0.1-harvest.md` (approved, built as 0.1.0; its Clarifications still apply). Report: `game/reports/2026-11-04-0.1-harvest.md`. Everything not named here stays as 0.1.0. Process: `game/CONTEXT.md` (contract check, Bob's shot set, snapshot tests, checkpoint commits on `milestone/0.1`).

## Changes

1. **Capped colour (designer: accepted).** The upright frame lights capped cells brighter than revision 3 did. Revision 3's figure is retired: the new reference is 0.1.0's measured capped colour (255, 198, 52) ±2 (and uncapped as measured in 0.1.0). The materials don't change.

2. **Hive lid lifts, then leans.** Today the lid slides straight to the ground and passes through the hive. Opening: the lid **lifts** straight up by `lid_lift_height` over `lid_lift_seconds`, then **tips and falls** over `lid_drop_seconds` to rest **leaning against the hive's side at an angle** (about 60–70° from flat, its lower edge on the ground), on the side away from the centrifuge and never between the camera and the risen frame. Closing plays the same path backwards. At no sampled point of either path may the lid's box overlap the hive body or a frame. The **centrifuge lid** gets the same lift first, so it no longer passes through the bucket; it still lands flat on the ground beside the bucket.
   - The open pose is the **full transform** (position and rotation) of `HiveLidSpot` / `CentrifugeLidSpot`, so Bob sets the leaning angle in the scene. Scripts tween position and rotation (a rotation tween only for the hive lid's fall; the centrifuge lid keeps its spin while on).

3. **Hive turned square to the centrifuge and resized** (designer's answers).
   - The hive **box** sits square to the ground grid like the crate and truck, so on screen it reads as a diamond (two side faces and the top showing), not one flat face toward the camera.
   - The **frames face the bottom-right of the screen** (face normal +X in world space, toward the centrifuge's crank side), standing in a row along Z like books.
   - **Footprint = the bucket's:** width and depth about 2.0 (the bucket's outer diameter); height is whatever the frames need, with the lid on top. Slot scale shrinks so 5 frames fit (frame width 7 cells at pitch 1.0 must fit inside about 1.8, so about 0.25).
   - **The zoom tightens** so every cell is still at least `min_cell_px` (44 px) wide **as seen** at the new angle (about 0.7 of face-on width). Bob sets `ZoomFraming` and `zoom_size` (config default updated to his value). The zoom still shows the hive with its risen frame and the bucket; BackButton never over either. If 44 px cannot be met with both in view, Bob reports the best width and the framing instead of guessing.
   - `frame_rise` is set so the risen frame clears the body with a visible gap (Bob reports the value).

4. **Every comb shows in the bucket.** The heap no longer stops at `bucket_visual_cap`: it shows every cell in the bucket, up to the capacity (`centrifuge_capacity_frames × cells_per_frame`; at 5 frames, 175 cells). The heap is laid out in layers so any capacity up to 5 frames fits inside the glass, and it melts as before. `bucket_visual_cap` is removed. The **pile** does the same: it shows every piled cell (`pile_visual_cap` removed); the count label stays.

5. **A real jar.** `jar.tscn` becomes a jar: a clear glass body (`glass.tres`), a lid (`JarLid`, steel), and honey inside (`JarHoney`, `honey_level.tres`). At the spigot the empty jar appears without its lid, the honey rises inside it over `bottling_seconds_per_jar`, then the lid goes on. Jars on the truck and in the crate are full, with lids.

6. **Wheel rims.** Each wheel gets a light-grey **rim** on its outer face with an off-centre mark (a contrasting stripe or bolt) so the roll is visible. The rims are children of the wheel, so they turn with it (0.1.0's roll from travel is unchanged).
