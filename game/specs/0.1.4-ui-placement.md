# 0.1.4 UI placement

**Builds on** `game/specs/0.1.3-next-revision.md` (layout, camera stops and speed row unchanged). Process: `game/CONTEXT.md`. Branch `milestone/0.1.4` from `milestone/0.1`, in the main folder (Process 10: no worktree unless work runs in parallel). **Uploaded to itch.io**: 0.1.4 is the A5 demo (see "Answered" at the bottom), and an assignment's demo build goes on itch even within a milestone (designer, 2026-11-06). Rex runs `build.py 0.1.4 --smoke` for the web zip, and the designer uploads it. The web smoke test is still unconfirmed (0.1.3 open item: the 100 s wait shows the loading spinner), so the designer checks the itch page loads before submitting.

**Sources:**
- The designer's 0.1.3 play-test (2026-11-06): "the ui is fucked, the back button isn't consistent, the spin and harvest button changes locations, and the 5x is overlaping with some other text in the top left".
- Round 7 votes on Design Votes (https://claude.ai/artifact/17i3YsGsf9zD7NowGxtDSa, `game/reports/ui-compare/decisions.json`). 0.1.3's layout is kept; the note is quoted in each item below.

## Changes

1. **Harvest button is the honey meter** (designer: "the honey meter should be transfered over to the harvest button so the background of it slowly fills up").
   - The top Hive bar is removed. The Harvest button is always shown; its background fills from the bottom with the hive's fill fraction, and it is pressable only when full (today's rule).
   - The hive count moves to just above the button as "7/10", without the word "Hive" (designer: "the hive text can go above the button, i.e 5/10 above the harvest button"; 2026-11-06: "no hive").
   - Bees and money stay at the top (designer: "the bees and money count can stay at the top").
   - Full state: the fill is complete and the button takes its present enabled look (STYLE_GUIDE tokens, unchanged).
   - **Play-test of 0.1.4 (designer, 2026-11-07):** "the meter loads but it is a rectangle in front of the button, so it's not cropped properly". The fill must sit behind the label and be clipped to the button's shape (rounded corners, inside its border), not drawn as a square rectangle on top of it.

2. **Buttons anchored to their props, the same way in every view** (play-test: "the spin and harvest button changes locations").
   - Each button follows one anchor point on its prop, projected to the screen every frame, in every camera view. No per-view offsets are allowed; today's runtime move of Spin out of FarmUI by `main.gd` goes away, because the scene places it.
   - **Harvest:** lower overall, just above the hive lid in the farm view (designer: "harvest button can be lower overall, in farm view just above the lid"). It is hidden at the second stop, where the lid may be off screen (designer, 2026-11-06: "hide it").
   - **Spin:** just above the centrifuge lid (designer: "same with spin button it can be just above the centrifuge lid").
   - **Sell:** lower, just above the truck bed between crate and truck, as in ab-A's `8-sell` (designer: "same with sell icon it can be lower, refer to ab-A for known good placement"; on ab-A: "sell icon is good here").
   - When an anchor would put a button off screen or over another control, the button is clamped inside the screen with a 16 px margin and nudged clear. Quinn's overlap check below catches failures.

3. **Back in one fixed place** (play-test: "the back button isn't consistent"). It is a screen-space button in the **top-right** corner in the first zoom and at the second stop, not found by a scan (designer, 2026-11-06: "top right to not interfear with the labels").

4. **Speed tag clear of the top info** (play-test: "the 5x is overlaping with some other text in the top left"). The ×N tag sits **under the money and bees lines** (designer, 2026-11-06).

5. **Bigger hive frames and combs** (designer: "hive frames and combs can be bigger to match the pile size"). The frames and their cells scale up so a cell reads at about the size of a cell in the bucket pile. The rack stays inside the hive body; the first zoom's ≥ 20 px rule and "hive + centrifuge whole" still hold.
   - **Design Votes round 8 (2026-11-07): all three variants rejected.** a: "frame model changed, frames too small"; b: "too small but close"; c: "hex are right size but again frame and hive model changed, unacceptable". The redo keeps the current hive and frame models exactly as they are and changes only sizes: variant c's hex cell size, frames bigger than b's. Milo renders new variants for a new Design Votes section; C5 is built from the pick. Fewer cells per row are allowed (designer, 2026-11-07: "fewer cells are ok"), e.g. Patricia's `grid_cols` 7 → 5 at about 1.35×, so the bigger frames fit the unchanged hive body.
   - **Design Votes round 9 (2026-11-07): variant a** (`game/scenes/variants/hive-frames/hive-frames-a.tscn`: real hive, lid and bars; frames 1.18× wider, 1.17 × 0.63; `grid_cols` 5 × `grid_rows` 2; cell radius 0.14). Note: "closest one, just needs more spacing between hexs, ok if pile hexs are smaller too so they should match the frame hex sizes". So: build a with more gap between cells (smaller cells are fine; overridden 2026-11-08, see item 14: combs "way too small"), and the pile and bucket cells take the same size as the frame cells. b and c (4×2, 3×2): "too empty".

6. **Housekeeping from 0.1.3:**
   - Tests and shot scripts never write into the player's `user://recordings` (about 278 test files landed there on 11/6). Under the tests, the recorder writes to a temp folder or is off.
   - The 10 X1_TESTS stay the designer's to play (D102); nothing here changes the timing tests.

7. **The pile loads on its own at the second stop** (designer's play-test of the merged build, 2026-11-06: "when in the second zoom screen the centrifuge fills up pours and then have to retap spin, this shouldn't be needed as we're already in the second zoom screen, the pile should just load up no matter if in the second screen or not").
   - When the centrifuge has finished a batch and poured, the waiting pile loads into it without a new Spin tap. This holds in every view, the second stop included.
   - Spin is needed only to start spinning a loaded centrifuge. The player is never asked to press Spin just to load the pile.
   - **What the designer saw (play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-07):** "the bucket pours then it loads the pile but prompts me to press spin again when already in spin mode, it should just keep the text while it loads again, if there's no pile it exits the spin mode". The pile does load; the bug is the prompt. While the pile reloads after a pour, spin mode stays on and its text stays as it was, with no "press Spin" prompt. If no pile is waiting after the pour, spin mode exits.

8. **Instruction text below Back** (designer's review of the 0.1.4 build, 2026-11-07: "back button overlaps instruction text at top, can fix by lowering instruction test below the back button").
   - The instruction text at the top of the zoom (`HintLabel`) moves down so it sits below Back's top-right rect, in the first zoom and at the second stop. Back stays where item 3 puts it.

9. **"Production paused" above the upgrades** (designer's play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-07: "in spin mode the production paused message is wrapping so let's move it above the upgrade section at the bottom"). `PausedLabel` ("Hive full, production paused", now in `TopInfo`) moves to just above the upgrade section at the bottom of the screen, on one line, in every view. Item 4 confirmed: the ×N tag displays correctly.

10. **Combs shown in the hive** (designer's play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-07: "the honeycombs aren't shown until the frame pops out"). The comb cells on each frame are visible while the frame sits in the hive, filling as the hive fills, not only once the frame is raised for harvest. Goes with item 5 (bigger frames): the rerun of C5 builds both.

   - **What the designer saw (play-test of the C5 build, 2026-11-08):** "the combs don't fill out the frame and there's a side missing to the hive". The comb cells must cover each frame's inner area edge to edge, and the hive body must be whole (all sides drawn) after the rescale. (Played while C5's fix round was running, so recheck against the finished C5.)

11. **Spin goes to the second stop** (designer's play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-08: "the spin button should bring it to zoom 2 view"). Pressing Spin from any view moves the camera to the second stop (the centrifuge zoom) and enters spin mode there. Confirmed 2026-11-08: "you shouldn't be able to spin from farm view, you have to re-tap spin to go back in to zoom and harvest". So the farm view never cranks: after leaving the second stop (Back, or the pile running out), Spin is tapped again to return, and this holds for a part-full bucket too. Kept as is (designer, 2026-11-08): Harvest stays hidden at the second stop (a full hive needs Back first), and Spin during the first zoom ends it with the unscraped frames left for the next Harvest.

12. **No spinning in the farm view without the motor** (designer's play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-08: "centrifuge shouldn't spin in farm view without the electric centrifuge upgrade, or in stage 1 of the game"). The centrifuge spins only at the second stop, cranked by the player. It may spin in the farm view (zoomed out) only once the **motor** (GDD Automation: "the electric centrifuge spins by itself") is owned, which never happens in Phase 1 ("stage 1" = Phase 1, designer confirmed 2026-11-08). This applies to the auto-reload after a pour too (`_auto_spin` in `main.gd`).

14. **The hive is the round 9 pick** (designer's play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-08: "the hive and frame models were changed WRONG"). C5 built a shallow hive body with its front-left wall missing and two large flat frames, not the pick. `scenes/props/hive.tscn` must match `scenes/variants/hive-frames/hive-frames-a.tscn` (render `game/reports/variants/hive-frames-r9/hive-frames-r9-a.png`): a deep box with all four walls, five upright frames standing side by side, the comb cells on each frame filling it edge to edge, the lid hinged at the back. The frames still rise one at a time in the first zoom.
   - **What the designer saw (play-test of the C14 build, 2026-11-08):** "in the game I'm testing the combs are way too small" and "the lid doesn't close properly". Confirmed by the designer: the combs are too small **when a frame is raised** (first zoom); the lid **clips through the hive body at an angle** when it closes. The comb size is already decided, no new vote: round 9 variant a (`hive-frames-a.tscn`: 5 × 2 cells, cell radius 0.14 on its frame, the cells filling the frame edge to edge with a visible gap between hexes; render `hive-frames-r9-a.png`), and a raised frame shows its cells at that proportion of the frame, not smaller (this overrides round 9's "smaller cells are fine" in item 5). The lid closes flat onto the top of the four walls, turning about its back hinge, and never passes through the body at any point of the animation.

15. **Leaving the centrifuge stops the crank** (designer's play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-08: "If I start spinning the centrifuge and back out I can still spin it WRONG"). Back at the second stop while cranking pauses the crank: no turn counts outside the second stop, and progress is kept. Spin shows again in the farm view while a crank is paused, and tapping it pans back to the second stop, where the crank resumes from where it stopped.

13. **Centrifuge text sits on the centrifuge** (designer's play-test of 0.1.4, 2026-11-08: "the centrifuge helper text should stay on top of the centrifgue not screen position locked"). `CentrifugeLabel` ("Honey: x / y" and the crank hint) is fixed at screen (24, 245) in `FarmUI` today. It follows the centrifuge's lid anchor through the same anchor-follow component as Harvest, Spin and Sell (item 2), sitting above the centrifuge in every view, clamped inside the screen and clear of Spin and Back.

**Shots of gradual animations** (designer, 2026-11-07, after the meter looked right in still shots but wrong in play): any moment that changes gradually (the Harvest fill, the pour, the pile loading) is captured at several points, at least empty, about half and full, not just at one point.

**Config and Dev mode** (Process 3): new live values in the Dev panel: `frame_scale` (start: whatever matches the pile cell, Bob measures), `harvest_anchor_offset`, `spin_anchor_offset`, `sell_anchor_offset` (world units above each anchor), `button_screen_margin` (16).

**Out of scope:** crate tiers and Deep Frame (Terry's numbers first), the onboarding script, a new camera stop.
