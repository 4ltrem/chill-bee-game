**Sources:** `GDD.md` (1, 2, 3 Tree A and B, 8, 9, Engine Integration, Technical Constraints), with the §2 changes below once accepted. The designer's play-test of revision 3 (2026-11-04). `research/hive-harvest-equipment.md` (Ruth, draft) for the real hive and extractor. **Terms.** The designer's **rack** is the GDD's and the code's **frame** (Ruth's sources say frame too). This spec says frame. ## Look: colorbox

- Primitive meshes and flat colours only, as before. No imported models, textures or audio.
- Sunny Meadow, the bees, the flowers, the jar crate and the truck as in revision 3.
- **Hive (two-piece):** a tan open-topped **body** box and a slightly wider, darker **lid** box on top. Inside the body, **5 frames** stand upright in a row, edge-on to each other like books, each a thin wooden-brown box (the frame bar) holding its cells. Only the risen frame's cells need to be readable. The lid drops to the ground beside the body (on the side away from the centrifuge) and goes back the same way.
- **Frame cells:** hexagonal prisms as now, but the frame stands upright: its face points toward the camera's horizontal direction, so the cells are seen at the isometric angle. `honeycomb.gd` builds its cells as now; the upright pose is a rotation of the frame node, so cell size, colours, heights and the pop are unchanged.
- **Centrifuge (two-piece, designer 2026-11-04):** a **glass bucket**: an open cylinder, see-through (`StandardMaterial3D` with alpha transparency, a pale blue-grey tint, `cull_mode` disabled so the far wall shows), on a steel base ring, with the **spigot** low on its side. A **steel lid** (a flat grey disc) carries the **crank** (arm and handle, turning with the spin). While the bucket is filling, the lid sits on the ground beside it; it drops on when the bucket closes. What shows through the glass:
  - **Filling:** the combs that flew in, as cell meshes heaped in the bucket (up to `bucket_visual_cap`), Capped-gold as they arrived.
  - **Cranking:** the combs **melt into honey**: as extraction progresses, the comb meshes shrink and fade, and a **honey level** (a gold, slightly emissive cylinder inside the glass) rises to match the jars extracted. At `DONE` only honey is left.
  - **Bottling:** the honey level **drops** jar by jar as each jar fills at the spigot, and is gone when the bucket is empty.
- **Piles:** cells that arrive while the bucket is closed land on the ground beside the centrifuge in piles: stacks of cell meshes, up to `pile_visual_cap` shown, with a count label (Label3D) beyond that.
- **Truck wheels:** the wheels turn while the truck drives, and their roll matches the direction of travel both ways (rolling forward driving off, backward reversing home, or forward if it turns round; whichever it does, no skid).
- The farm camera as now. **The zoom:** the farm `Camera3D` tweens its position and `size` to a framing where the hive (with its risen frame) and the centrifuge fill most of the portrait screen, over `zoom_seconds`, and back the same way. No panning.

**Colorbox simplifications of GDD §2:** no honey streams or particles (the bucket spins); the truck slides; no audio or haptics; the cell's flight is a simple arc tween.

## The loop

1. **Bees forage, hive fills.** The hive fills its frames in order, cell by cell: each cell is worth `frame_jars / cells_per_frame` jars (2 / 35), and an empty slot becomes a Capped cell when enough honey arrives. The top info shows money, the fill bar ("Hive: n / 10" in jars) and the bee counter. The hive is **full** when all 5 frames are full. Past that, honey goes to the overflow buffer (one hive, 10 jars); when that is full too, production pauses and the paused label shows. As slots empty, overflow honey pours back into them. **A frame that is risen (on display) never fills**; honey goes to the other frames or the overflow.
2. **Fork icon.** It shows when the hive is full, whatever the centrifuge is doing. After a Back (step 3), it also shows while any frame has a Capped cell, until a zoom finishes on its own. It is hidden during the zoom.
3. **Stage 1, uncapping (the zoom).** Tapping the fork: the farm UI hides (top info, icons, shop, Continue, the centrifuge label), the camera zooms in, the hive lid drops to the ground, and the first frame with Capped cells slides up. Drag to uncap, with raycasts from the farm camera onto the cells' colliders as now.
   - **Each scraped cell** goes Capped → Uncapped and pops **exactly as in revision 3** (`honeycomb.gd`'s `_pop`: scale 1.3 → 1 over 0.18 s, back-out). After the pop it flies on an arc over `cell_fly_seconds` to the bucket if the bucket is open and has room, otherwise to the pile. Its slot is empty from then on (state Extracted, no mesh). The jar value moves from the hive to the bucket or pile at the moment of the scrape.
   - **Bucket full:** when the bucket holds `centrifuge_capacity_frames × cells_per_frame` cells, its lid drops on (`lid_drop_seconds`); later cells pile up.
   - **Frame done:** when a frame has no Capped cells, it slides down (`slide_seconds`) and the next frame with Capped cells slides up. The zoom works through the frames that had Capped cells when it opened.
   - **Zoom ends on its own** when no frame is left: the hive lid goes back on, the camera zooms out (after `close_delay_seconds`), the farm UI returns.
   - **Back** (`BackButton`, top corner, never over the frame or the bucket): the risen frame slides down **keeping its state**, the hive lid goes back on, and the camera zooms out. The fork stays available while any frame has Capped cells.
   - **When the zoom ends either way** and the bucket is open with cells in it, its lid goes on.
   - During the zoom: crank input never counts (a drag on a frame is never a crank turn), cranking stops counting until the zoom ends, and bottling, the truck and the hive fill keep running.
4. **Centrifuge closed.** With its lid on, the **centrifuge icon** ("Spin") floats above it. Nothing spins until the player taps it.
5. **Stage 2, extraction (on the farm).** As revision 3 (tap or hold the crank, the centrifuge or the screen around it; the tap cap; hold counts one turn on press, then `max_tap_rate`/s; the visual ramp), except the turns needed are `ceil(jars_in_bucket × taps_per_jar)`. The label shows "Honey: n / m" in jars, rounded down. Done: the spin winds down and **Continue** appears.
6. **Stage 3, bottling.** As revision 3: one jar every `bottling_seconds_per_jar` to the truck, or the crate while the truck is away. A part-full bucket bottles `floor(jars)` jars; the leftover fraction carries over into the next batch (it is never lost). The summary then shows, and the **lid lifts off** to the ground: the bucket is empty and open.
7. **Piles load in.** When the bucket becomes empty and open, pile cells move into it by themselves (one flight per visible cell, or one tween of the pile), up to its capacity. If that fills the bucket, or no zoom is open, the lid goes on and step 4 follows. If a zoom is open, the bucket stays open for the cells still coming.
8. **Sell** and **Upgrade** as revision 3. The shop works whenever the farm UI shows (not during the zoom).
9. Round again.

**Centrifuge states** (revision 3's table, with `LOADING` added):

| State | Lid | Cells arrive | Crank input counts | Continue | Centrifuge icon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `EMPTY` | off | to the bucket | no | hidden | hidden |
| `LOADING` (cells in, room left) | off | to the bucket | no | hidden | hidden |
| `FULL` (lid on, waiting) | on | to the pile | no | hidden | shown (farm only) |
| `CRANKING` | on | to the pile | **yes, unless zoomed** | hidden | hidden |
| `DONE` | on | to the pile | no | shown (farm only) | hidden |
| `BOTTLING` | on | to the pile | no | hidden | hidden |

The fork icon follows step 2 at every state; the Sell icon follows the truck; nothing on the farm UI shows during the zoom except `BackButton` and the hint.

Out of scope: as revision 3 (the Long Fork and every other upgrade, automation, Auto-Sell, grid expansion, free panning and zoom, biomes, Habitat, pests, offline earnings, the guide, saving, localisation, audio, haptics, particles, monetisation).

## Patricia's confirmation (0.1)

Written before the scripts, so Bob can build from it. Every name in "Node paths for Bob" is **confirmed unchanged**, with the additions and choices below (marked **my choice** where the spec was silent or two numbers conflicted). Scripts find parts by node name with `find_child(name, true, false)`, so a part may sit at any depth inside its prop scene; only the **names**, the **axes** and the **no-collider** rules below matter. A missing scene, material or node is skipped with one `push_warning`, never a crash.

### Conventions every prop scene follows

- Units are metres of the farm; Y is up. The camera is isometric from (+X, +Y, +Z) looking toward -X, -Z. Its horizontal direction toward the camera is `d = (0.7071, 0, 0.7071)`; camera right is `(0.7071, 0, -0.7071)`.
- **Lids:** the lid node's position in its scene is its **closed pose** (on the hive body, on the bucket). The `HiveLidSpot` / `CentrifugeLidSpot` marker's position (in the root's local space) is the exact **origin of the open pose** on the ground, so Bob places the marker with the lid's own origin height already included. Scripts tween the lid's `position` between the two (`lid_drop_seconds`) and never rotate or scale it, except that the centrifuge's `Lid` is spun about its own Y while it is on.
- **No collider anywhere** on: both lids, the heap cells, pile cells, flying cells, glass, honey level, spigot. The only colliders in the 3D scene are the `Body` of each cell of the frame inside `honey_cell.tscn` (scripts free `Body` from every cell used for a heap, pile or flight).
- Materials: scripts only `load()` the four cell materials (below). Everything else, Bob assigns in the scene.

### `honey_cell.tscn` (one hexagonal cell)

### `hive.tscn` (root `Hive`, script `res://scripts/hive.gd`, group `bee_home`)

```
Hive                  Node3D   script hive.gd
  HiveBody            MeshInstance3D (or a group) tan, open top, sized to hold 5 frames
  HiveLid             Node3D   slightly wider, darker; no collider; its scene position = closed pose
  HiveLidSpot         Marker3D open pose origin (ground, beside the body, away from the centrifuge)
  ForkAnchor          Marker3D above the hive
  FrameSlots          Node3D
    Slot1 .. Slot5    Marker3D one per frame, at the frame's LOWERED pose inside the body
  FrameBars           Node3D
    FrameBar1 .. FrameBar5  Node3D, each with the wooden frame (thin boxes, wood.tres)
  Honeycomb           Node3D   script res://scripts/honeycomb.gd; no transform needed
```

- **Slot axes (what I rely on).** A slot's transform is copied to `Honeycomb` and to the matching `FrameBar` when that frame is raised, **including its scale**. In slot-local space: **+Y is the frame's face normal (the side the cells show on, toward the camera)**, **+X is the frame's width (cell columns)**, **+Z is the frame's height, pointing DOWN** (so row 0 is at the top). For the isometric camera: basis X = `(0.7071, 0, -0.7071)`, Y = `(0.7071, 0, 0.7071)`, Z = `(0, -1, 0)` (any equivalent basis is fine). The frame is 7 wide by 3.6 high at scale 1 (pitch 1.0, rows 0.9), so Bob sets the slots' **uniform scale** (0.3 to 0.4 is likely) so the body holds five; colliders and cells scale with it. The five slots differ only in position (stacked along `d`, edge-on like books).
- **FrameBar**: its node transform is **identical to its slot's**, so Bob models the bar in slot-local units: a thin wooden border and a back plate just behind the cells (cells sit at local Y 0 to 0.3). When frame i rises, `FrameBar_i` and `Honeycomb` move together.
- **Rise direction** is the slot's frame-up, `-Slot.basis.z` (world up), by `frame_rise` (config, in the hive root's units). Bob checks that 1.2 clears the frame out of the body; if not, he reports the number and I set `frame_rise` in the config.
- `Honeycomb` is hidden whenever no frame is risen, and holds the cell colliders (Bob must not place anything with a collider between the camera and the risen frame).
- The hive root is not scaled (`HiveLidSpot`, slots and the rise are in its local space).

  Base                 MeshInstance3D  steel base ring (static)
  Spinner              Node3D          at the origin; the script spins it about Y while cranking
    Bucket             MeshInstance3D  open cylinder, glass.tres, outer radius about 1.0, height about 1.4, cull disabled

    HeapRoot           Marker3D        inner floor centre; the comb heap spawns under it. Scale it so a disc of radius 3.5 local units fits the glass
  Lid                  Node3D          steel disc and its crank; scene position = closed pose (on the bucket); no collider

- At run time the lid **starts in its open pose** (the state starts `EMPTY`, lid off): in `_ready` the script remembers `Lid`'s scene position as the closed pose, then moves `Lid` to `CentrifugeLidSpot`. The hive lid starts closed.

They use `cell_capped` until the first crank turn, then `comb_melting`, shrinking with the melt.

### Choices that fill gaps in the spec

- Flying cells look **Uncapped** (glowing) in flight, then **Capped-gold** in the heap or pile (**my choice**).
- Logic never waits for tweens: a scrape moves its jar value at once, lids and frames change state at the call (signals fire then), and `main.gd`'s zoom sequence runs on timers in `advance(delta)`. Tweens only draw the motion. The cell's flight starts 0.18 s after the scrape (the pop), driven by `honeycomb.advance(delta)`.
- The bucket's lid state closes **at the scrape that fills it**; its drawn drop waits `cell_fly_seconds` so it does not land on cells in flight.
- Extra config values beyond the Values table (all placeholders): `cell_fly_arc` (flight arc height, 1.5 units), `pile_load_stagger_seconds` (0.03 s between pile cells loading in).
- A leftover Dev mode file `user://dev_config.tres` from revision 3 holds old values (`frame_jars = 10`, `centrifuge_capacity_frames = 1`) and overrides the new defaults: press **Reset to defaults** in the Dev panel once.

## Clarifications (2026-11-04, from Quinn's spec-first tests)

Settled by the orchestrator within the approved spec; they take precedence over anything above that reads differently.

- **Jars come from integer cell counts.** Every jar figure (bucket, pile, leftover, turns needed) is computed as `cells × frame_jars / cells_per_frame` from an integer cell count, never by adding up per-cell floats: 35 cells fed one at a time must give exactly 2 jars and 8 turns.
- **API kept from revision 3:** `begin_crank()`, `open_spigot()`, `turns_counted`, `state_changed(state)`, `extraction_done`, `jar_bottled(count)`, `bottling_done(jars)`; new fields `cells`, `jars`, `leftover_jars`, `lid_open`. `jars_total` and `jars_extracted` are floats in jars. `fill()` is gone (`add_cells()` replaces it).
- **Scraped cell state:** `hive.scrape()` writes state 2 (Extracted) at once (logic never waits for tweens); the Uncapped look, the pop and the flight are visual only. A scraped slot's collider is disabled.
- **`raise_frame(i)` on a frame with no Capped cells** is allowed; it rises showing empty slots and never fills while risen.
- **Overflow pours back at once** into slots as they empty (except the risen frame's), not at the fill rate.
- **`has_room()` is false while the lid is on**, whatever the cell count.
- **The zoom's queue is fixed when it opens:** a frame that refills during the zoom is not brought up again in the same zoom.
- **Slots fill in index order** (cell 0, row 0 at the top, first).
- **Fork when the hive is not full:** hidden, unless a Back set `resume_pending` and a Capped cell remains.
- **Placeholder marks:** every new config value is marked `# PLACEHOLDER (GDD TBD)` except `min_cell_px` (a check).
- **Pixel tolerance:** the upright frame's capped vs uncapped colours are compared with revision 3's ±2 per channel; a miss is reported as a finding, not a widened tolerance.
