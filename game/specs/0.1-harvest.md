# 0.1 Harvest: the manual core loop (colorbox)

**Sources:** `GDD.md` (1, 2, 3 Tree A and B, 8, 9, Engine Integration, Technical Constraints), with the §2 changes below once accepted. The designer's play-test of revision 3 (2026-11-04). `research/hive-harvest-equipment.md` (Ruth, draft) for the real hive and extractor. It continues Prototype 0, closed at revision 3 (`game/specs/prototype-0.md`); its report is `game/reports/2026-11-04-prototype-0-r3.md`.

**Terms.** The designer's **rack** is the GDD's and the code's **frame** (Ruth's sources say frame too). This spec says frame. The hive **body** is the box that holds the frames, and the hive **lid** sits on top. The centrifuge **bucket** is the drum with the spigot, and the centrifuge **lid** carries the crank.

## Look: colorbox

- Primitive meshes and flat colours only, as before. No imported models, textures or audio.
- Sunny Meadow, the bees, the flowers, the jar crate and the truck as in revision 3.
- **Hive (two-piece):** a tan open-topped **body** box and a slightly wider, darker **lid** box on top. Inside the body, **5 frames** stand upright in a row, edge-on to each other like books, each a thin wooden-brown box (the frame bar) holding its cells. Only the risen frame's cells need to be readable. The lid drops to the ground beside the body (on the side away from the centrifuge) and goes back the same way.
- **Frame cells:** hexagonal prisms as now, but the frame stands upright: its face points toward the camera's horizontal direction, so the cells are seen at the isometric angle. `honeycomb.gd` builds its cells as now; the upright pose is a rotation of the frame node, so cell size, colours, heights and the pop are unchanged.
- **Centrifuge (two-piece, designer 2026-11-04):** a **glass bucket**: an open cylinder, see-through (`StandardMaterial3D` with alpha transparency, a pale blue-grey tint, `cull_mode` disabled so the far wall shows), on a steel base ring, with the **spigot** low on its side. A **steel lid** (a flat grey disc) carries the **crank** (arm and handle, turning with the spin). While the bucket is filling, the lid sits on the ground beside it; it drops on when the bucket closes. What shows through the glass:
  - **Filling:** the combs that flew in, as cell meshes heaped in the bucket (up to `bucket_visual_cap`), Capped-gold as they arrived.
  - **Cranking:** the combs **melt into honey**: as extraction progresses, the comb meshes shrink and fade, and a **honey level** (a gold, slightly emissive cylinder inside the glass) rises to match the jars extracted. At `DONE` only honey is left.
  - **Bottling:** the honey level **drops** jar by jar as each jar fills at the spigot, and is gone when the bucket is empty.
- **Piles:** cells that arrive while the bucket is closed land on the ground beside the centrifuge in piles: stacks of cell meshes, up to `pile_visual_cap` shown, with a count label (Label3D) beyond that.
- **Truck wheels:** the wheels turn while the truck drives, and their roll matches the direction of travel both ways (rolling forward driving off, backward reversing home, or forward if it turns round; whichever it does, no skid).
- The farm camera as now. **The zoom:** the farm `Camera3D` tweens its position and `size` to a framing where the hive (with its risen frame) and the centrifuge fill most of the portrait screen, over `zoom_seconds`, and back the same way. No panning.

**Colorbox simplifications of GDD §2:** no honey streams or particles (the bucket spins); the truck slides; no audio or haptics; the cell's flight is a simple arc tween.

## The loop

1. **Bees forage, hive fills.** The hive fills its frames in order, cell by cell: each cell is worth `frame_jars / cells_per_frame` jars (2 / 35), and an empty slot becomes a Capped cell when enough honey arrives. The top info shows money, the fill bar ("Hive: n / 10" in jars) and the bee counter. The hive is **full** when all 5 frames are full. Past that, honey goes to the overflow buffer (one hive, 10 jars); when that is full too, production pauses and the paused label shows. As slots empty, overflow honey pours back into them. **A frame that is risen (on display) never fills**; honey goes to the other frames or the overflow.
2. **Fork icon.** It shows when the hive is full, whatever the centrifuge is doing. After a Back (step 3), it also shows while any frame has a Capped cell, until a zoom finishes on its own. It is hidden during the zoom.
3. **Stage 1, uncapping (the zoom).** Tapping the fork: the farm UI hides (top info, icons, shop, Continue, the centrifuge label), the camera zooms in, the hive lid drops to the ground, and the first frame with Capped cells slides up. Drag to uncap, with raycasts from the farm camera onto the cells' colliders as now.
   - **Each scraped cell** goes Capped → Uncapped and pops **exactly as in revision 3** (`honeycomb.gd`'s `_pop`: scale 1.3 → 1 over 0.18 s, back-out). After the pop it flies on an arc over `cell_fly_seconds` to the bucket if the bucket is open and has room, otherwise to the pile. Its slot is empty from then on (state Extracted, no mesh). The jar value moves from the hive to the bucket or pile at the moment of the scrape.
   - **Bucket full:** when the bucket holds `centrifuge_capacity_frames × cells_per_frame` cells, its lid drops on (`lid_drop_seconds`); later cells pile up.
   - **Frame done:** when a frame has no Capped cells, it slides down (`slide_seconds`) and the next frame with Capped cells slides up. The zoom works through the frames that had Capped cells when it opened.
   - **Zoom ends on its own** when no frame is left: the hive lid goes back on, the camera zooms out (after `close_delay_seconds`), the farm UI returns.
   - **Back** (`BackButton`, top corner, never over the frame or the bucket): the risen frame slides down **keeping its state**, the hive lid goes back on, and the camera zooms out. The fork stays available while any frame has Capped cells.
   - **When the zoom ends either way** and the bucket is open with cells in it, its lid goes on.
   - During the zoom: crank input never counts (a drag on a frame is never a crank turn), cranking stops counting until the zoom ends, and bottling, the truck and the hive fill keep running.
4. **Centrifuge closed.** With its lid on, the **centrifuge icon** ("Spin") floats above it. Nothing spins until the player taps it.
5. **Stage 2, extraction (on the farm).** As revision 3 (tap or hold the crank, the centrifuge or the screen around it; the tap cap; hold counts one turn on press, then `max_tap_rate`/s; the visual ramp), except the turns needed are `ceil(jars_in_bucket × taps_per_jar)`. The label shows "Honey: n / m" in jars, rounded down. Done: the spin winds down and **Continue** appears.
6. **Stage 3, bottling.** As revision 3: one jar every `bottling_seconds_per_jar` to the truck, or the crate while the truck is away. A part-full bucket bottles `floor(jars)` jars; the leftover fraction carries over into the next batch (it is never lost). The summary then shows, and the **lid lifts off** to the ground: the bucket is empty and open.
7. **Piles load in.** When the bucket becomes empty and open, pile cells move into it by themselves (one flight per visible cell, or one tween of the pile), up to its capacity. If that fills the bucket, or no zoom is open, the lid goes on and step 4 follows. If a zoom is open, the bucket stays open for the cells still coming.
8. **Sell** and **Upgrade** as revision 3. The shop works whenever the farm UI shows (not during the zoom).
9. Round again.

**Centrifuge states** (revision 3's table, with `LOADING` added):

| State | Lid | Cells arrive | Crank input counts | Continue | Centrifuge icon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `EMPTY` | off | to the bucket | no | hidden | hidden |
| `LOADING` (cells in, room left) | off | to the bucket | no | hidden | hidden |
| `FULL` (lid on, waiting) | on | to the pile | no | hidden | shown (farm only) |
| `CRANKING` | on | to the pile | **yes, unless zoomed** | hidden | hidden |
| `DONE` | on | to the pile | no | shown (farm only) | hidden |
| `BOTTLING` | on | to the pile | no | hidden | hidden |

The fork icon follows step 2 at every state; the Sell icon follows the truck; nothing on the farm UI shows during the zoom except `BackButton` and the hint.

Out of scope: as revision 3 (the Long Fork and every other upgrade, automation, Auto-Sell, grid expansion, free panning and zoom, biomes, Habitat, pests, offline earnings, the guide, saving, localisation, audio, haptics, particles, monetisation).

## Models as reusable scenes (designer, 2026-11-04)

Only the **hive** and the **centrifuge** get new models. Every other model is kept as it is, but **moved out of the scripts into its own scene** under `game/scenes/props/`, so it can be reused (more hives and trucks later, other biomes) and swapped for an asset-pack model without touching code. A script keeps the behaviour and finds its model's parts by node name.

| Scene (`scenes/props/`) | What it holds | Today built by |
|---|---|---|
| `truck.tscn` | the pickup: cab, bed, four wheels (`WheelFL`, `WheelFR`, `WheelRL`, `WheelRR`), `BedJars` marker; root has `truck.gd` | `truck.gd` `_build_pickup()` |
| `jar.tscn` | one amber jar | `truck.gd` and `centrifuge.gd` (two copies of the same mesh) |
| `jar_crate.tscn` | the crate box and its `CrateJars` marker | `main.tscn` + `truck.gd` `_build_crate_jars()` |
| `flower_patch.tscn` | one flower patch (group `flower_patch`), colour as an exported var | `main.tscn` (three hand-made copies) |
| `hive.tscn` (**new model**) | body, lid, five frame slots, `ForkAnchor`, `HiveLidSpot`; root has `hive.gd` | `main.tscn` `HiveBox` |
| `centrifuge.tscn` (**new model**) | glass bucket, steel lid with crank, spigot, honey level, `CentrifugeAnchor`, `CentrifugeLidSpot`, `BucketMouth`; root has `centrifuge.gd` | `centrifuge.gd` `_build_*` |
| `honey_cell.tscn` | one hexagonal cell (mesh + collider), shared by the frame, the flying cell, the bucket and the piles | `honeycomb.gd` `_build_cells()` |

- Materials go in `game/materials/` as `.tres` (capped, uncapped, extracted, jar amber, glass, steel, wood), shared by every scene, so the A6 style checks have one palette to read.
- `main.tscn` instances these scenes; the node names in "Node paths for Bob" are unchanged.
- **The cell look and the pop stay exactly as now** when the cell moves into `honey_cell.tscn`: same mesh sizes, materials, `STATE_Y` heights, and the `_pop` tween. Quinn's pixel check of capped vs uncapped must give the same numbers as revision 3 (±2 per channel).
- The bees stay a `MultiMesh` built by `bees.gd` (a scene per bee would cost frames).
