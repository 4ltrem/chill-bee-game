5. **An adversarial QA pass** (MILESTONES M5). Quinn tries to break the 0.3.2 build on purpose through real input, at 720 × 1280 and with `show_hint` both off and on, and writes one test per break found. The attacks, at least: two taps on the same icon in one frame and in two frames (Harvest, Spin, Sell, Shop, Journal, Settings, Tools, Back, Skip, a Buy); a tap while the camera moves; Back at every step of the harvest (uncapping, the camera's travel, the crank, bottling); app pause and resume at every step (`NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_PAUSED`: input stops counting, automatic work finishes, `game/CONTEXT.md` rule 2); the locale switched with each sheet or panel open; a purchase with exactly its price in money and with one less; every sheet and panel opened, then a toast queued, then closed; the victory reached with each panel open. Quinn's report lists each attack, PASS or the break found; each break's test fails until its owner fixes it in a fix round. A break whose right behaviour the spec doesn't say is a question for the designer, not a guess.

6. **Bottom slot A: quick-buy, with a Dev switch** (`ui-screens.md` §3 option A; designer 2026-11-23: "A only, B-D later"). Config `bottom_slot` (int, default 1): 0 = nothing at the bottom (today), 1 = A; 2–4 stay free for B–D. With `shop_layout = 1` and `bottom_slot = 1`, a `QuickBuy` bar under `FarmUI` sits at the bottom of the farm view, left of the `ShopButton`: the next upgrade's name, its price and a Buy button (`QuickBuyButton`, at least 120 px tall, V11; 28 px text, V12). The next upgrade is the Shop row (the eight, in Shop order) that is unlocked, not at its max and has the lowest price; a tie goes to the earlier row. The name, price and Buy text are the Shop row's own (the same `tr()` keys and the French price order, no new key). Buy runs the Shop's own purchase code (money, level, effects, toasts, the patch's Health and thriving), and the bar then shows the next upgrade. The bar shows only on the uncovered farm view, so a toast its purchase queues (`toast_patch_planted`, `toast_bumble_visitor`) shows at once through the 0.3.1 queue. Buy is greyed when you can't afford it. When no row is left, the bar hides. It shows on the farm view only: hidden in both zooms, while `any_panel_open()` (0.3.1 item 7), under the welcome and victory overlays. It clears the `ShopButton`, the `JournalButton`, the Sell anchor, the hive count, the toasts' place and the hint bubble. A patch planted from the bar ends the hint as one planted from the Shop. With `shop_layout = 0` the compact panel stays and the bar never shows. Dev button "Bottom slot" switches between 0 and 1 and shows the current value. Size and position are placeholders the designer confirms at play-test.

7. **A preview on every upgrade row** (canvas sticky: "Need previews for the upgrade screen, similar to build screen"; designer 2026-11-23: "Milo variants first"). **First, a vote:** Milo makes two colorbox variants (designer, 2026-11-23: "fewer variants") (primitive-only, the approved palette, V4) of a small model for each of the eight upgrades: Swarm, Flight Endurance, Heated Fork, Centrifuge Gearing, Spigot Flow, Faster Delivery, Auto-Sell, Plant wildflower patch. Where a prop already shows the upgrade in the game (the fork, the gearing, the spigot, the truck, the flower patch), a variant may be that prop simplified, with the same parts named. They go to `game/scenes/variants/preview-<id>-r1/`; `model_shots.py` shoots them and `compare_page.py add` puts them on Design Votes as one section `upgrade-previews` (yes / maybe / no per variant). **Stop:** the designer's pick. **Then the build:** Bob promotes each picked variant into `game/scenes/props/previews/<id>.tscn`. Each Shop row and Tools row gets a `PreviewTile` at its left, in the Build board's style (white tile, ink border, rounded corners; 144 × 144 at 720 × 1280), showing its upgrade's model, rendered once (a `SubViewport` with `render_target_update_mode` once, or a shared one), not every frame. The row's name, effect line and Buy keep their order to the right; rows still don't overlap and every row of the selected tab stays on screen at 720 × 1280 (the Hive tab's five rows the most). The quick-buy bar (item 6) shows its upgrade's preview too, at the bar's height. Size is a placeholder the designer confirms at play-test.
