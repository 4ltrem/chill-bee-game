# 0.3.3 Play-test fixes from 0.3.2

**Builds on** `game/specs/0.3.2-tests-and-quick-buy.md` (items 6, 7, 9 and 14 are its items). Process: `game/CONTEXT.md`. On `main`, after 0.3.2 is built and play-tested. **Played locally:** not uploaded to itch (a revision, not a new milestone number).

**Numbering:** the items keep their 0.3.2 numbers (12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19), so the play-test notes and the cross-references between them still match; "item 6", "item 7", "item 9" and "item 14" mean the 0.3.2 spec's items.

**Sources:** the designer's 2026-11-26 play-test of the 0.3.2 Windows build (quotes in each item); `research/web-loading-screen.md` (item 17).

**Not in this part:** the loading screen (0.3.2 item 17, built 2026-11-26); the Close X, spinning with the lid off and the PNG icons in the tiles (0.3.2 items 10, 11 and 14).

## Changes

12. **What each upgrade does, on its row** (designer, 2026-11-26, play-test: "the benefits of the upgrades aren't listed anywhere on the shop screen so would need a description section and also a little note exemple +15% hive filling speed"; picked: in each row). Today a Shop or Tools row has no description or note line (its text is the name, `LevelLabel` and Buy). After this change each row also shows, under its name, a one-line description (`RowDesc`, key `shop_desc_<id>`) and a small note (`RowNote`, key `shop_note_<id>`) giving what the next level adds, with its number read from config, not written into the text:
   - Worker Swarm Count: "+%d%% hive filling speed" (`swarm_fill_bonus_per_level`);
   - Flight Endurance: "+%d%% bee speed" (`flight_speed_bonus_per_level`);
   - Heated Uncapping Fork: "+%d%% fork reach" (`heated_fork_radius_bonus_per_level`);
   - Centrifuge Gearing: "−%d%% crank turns" (`gearing_cut_per_level`);
   - Spigot Flow: "−%d%% bottling time" (`spigot_flow_cut_per_level`);
   - Faster Delivery: "−%d%% truck trip time" (`faster_delivery_trip_cut_per_level`);
   - Auto-Sell: "Sells full crates by itself" (one-time, no number);
   - Plant wildflower patch: "+%d Habitat Health" (`habitat_health_per_patch`).
   At max level the note hides. Both lines go through `tr()` (French in `translations/`, "vous", prices "%d $"), so a language switch updates them; the quick-buy bar's label shows the same note (item 18). Note text 22 px, description 24 px, both ink on the sheet. Rows still don't overlap, and every row of the selected tab, with item 7's preview tiles, stays on screen at 720 × 1280 (the Hive tab's five rows the most). **Text and sizes are placeholders** (designer, 2026-11-26: "add to play-test notes"): the description wording, the note wording and the French are written as placeholders in the build and settled by the designer at the 0.3.3 play-test.

13. **The fork comes out of the Harvest circle and goes back into it** (designer, 2026-11-26, play-test: "the hive harvest icon should be spawning the 3d model that the player has to drag across the combs to collect and when the tap is released it goes back into the circle"). Supersedes `0.2-first-automation.md` item 10's "it hides when the press ends" and `main.gd`'s rule that `ForkIcon` shows on the farm view only. Today `ForkIcon` hides in the first zoom; on a press the fork prop appears at the drag point over the combs and hides on release, and only at the zoom's end does a fork still on screen shrink into the icon (`_return_fork_to_icon`). After this change `ForkIcon` also shows in the first zoom (not at the second stop), clear of `BackButton`, Spin, the Shop and Tools icons, the hive count and the hint bubble; its place there is a placeholder the designer confirms at play-test. On a press over the combs, the fork grows out of the circle's centre and travels to the drag point over `fork_return_seconds`, then follows the drag and uncaps as today (cells uncap only once it has arrived). On release, wherever it lands, it flies back to the circle and shrinks into it over `fork_return_seconds`, then hides; a new press during the return starts it out again from where it is. The zoom's end keeps today's return. While the fork is out, the circle shows empty (no fork glyph); the honey fill stays.

15. **The fork uncaps under its tines, not under the pointer** (designer, 2026-11-26, play-test: "the hitbox of the fork should be around the model not the cursor"; picked: the tines' footprint). Today `honeycomb.gd` uncaps the cell under the pointer's ray plus every capped cell within `reach_radius()` of it. After this change the cells uncapped are those under the fork's head where it sits over the comb (the tines' width across, as drawn), so what the player sees the fork touch is what uncaps; Heated Fork reach widens that footprint as it widened the radius. With item 13, nothing uncaps while the fork is still travelling out.

16. **A hint for the Habitat bar** (designer, 2026-11-26, play-test: "a bit should be added in the hints that to restore the habitat the meter should be full, you can use some of the same honey highlight around the bar"). Then (designer, 2026-11-26: "it should be part of the intro sequence"). Today the first-time hint walks `hint_wait` … `hint_plant` and ends at the first patch. Then (designer, 2026-11-26: "it should be the first thing that's shown more or less (after the character intro, part of the explanation of why the player is here and how they can restore the meadow)"). After this change the walk opens with a new step, `hint_habitat` (placeholder: "The meadow is fading. Fill the Habitat bar to bring the wild bees back!" / FR placeholder), first in `STEP_KEYS`, before `hint_wait`: it shows as soon as the welcome overlay closes, its tail pointing at `HabitatPill` (item 9), and ends on a tap on the bubble or after `hint_habitat_seconds` (config, placeholder 6, live in Dev), counted only while the bubble shows (designer, 2026-11-26: it pauses while a sheet or panel covers it or the pill is hidden in a zoom, so the line is always read); the walk then goes on from `hint_wait` as today. While it shows, a honey-coloured glow pulses around the bar, the same colour and pulse as the Harvest circle's at full; the glow ends with the step. Skip ends the whole walk as today. Wording a placeholder the designer settles at play-test.

17. **Frame rate on the web build** (designer, 2026-11-26, play-test: "the fps is lower on itch.io"). Quinn profiles the web build (`build.py --smoke`, then a real browser at 720 × 1280) against the 60 FPS target on the farm view, both zooms and the open Shop, and his report names what costs the frames (e.g. shadows, the bee count, `SubViewport`s, the pile). The fixes are a fix round from that report, each with its before/after FPS; a fix that changes the look is a question for the designer. The report also states whether itch serves `index.wasm` and `index.pck` compressed (the `Content-Encoding` header in the browser's Network tab, with the load time; `research/web-loading-screen.md`).

18. **The quick-buy label says what the upgrade does** (designer, 2026-11-26, play-test: "replace the duplicate label keep the price on the button and have the label next to it describe the effect of the upgrade"). The price shows on `QuickBuyButton` only, once item 6 removes `QuickBuyPrice`. Today `QuickBuyName` shows the next upgrade's name. After this change it shows that upgrade's effect: the same text as its Shop row's `RowNote` (item 12's `shop_note_<id>` through `tr()`, its number from config), so a language switch updates it and it changes when the bar moves to the next upgrade. No new translation key. The text is a placeholder, settled with item 12's notes at the 0.3.3 play-test.
19. **A wider spigot per level, and icons that show the next level** (designer, 2026-11-26, play-test: "the spigot upgrade should make the hole wider"; then "the icon should show the next upgrade preview until the max one which is just the final look"). Today the `Spigot` (a 0.12-radius cylinder, `centrifuge.tscn:100`) looks the same at every Spigot Flow level, and each upgrade has one icon (item 14). After this change the `Spigot`'s radius grows with the level: x and z scale = 1 + `spigot_widen_per_level` × level (new config, placeholder 0.1: 1.5× at level 5), its tip still at the bucket wall; the jar still lands under it. The Heated Fork icon shows why the upgrade is better (designer, 2026-11-26: "the fork icon should have a hitbox radius to visualize why it's better"): `uncap_fork.gd` gets a `ReachRing`, a flat translucent ring under the fork's head sized to its uncap footprint at the level (item 15's footprint; today `uncap_radius` × (1 + `heated_fork_radius_bonus_per_level` × level) cell pitches), hidden in play and shown when `show_reach` is true (the icon recipe sets it) or while the Dev panel's "Show UI bounds" is on (`dev_panel.gd:181` `set_ui_bounds`; designer, 2026-11-26: "the ring should be visible in the ui bounds mode of the dev panel"), so the fork's real reach can be checked while uncapping. For every upgrade whose look differs by level (Spigot Flow by this item; Centrifuge Gearing, gear count level + 1; Faster Delivery, the truck's stacks, decals and flames, `truck.gd:246`; Heated Fork, its `ReachRing`), `model_snap.py --recipe upgrades` writes one icon per level, `<id>_<level>.png` (levels 1 to max, shot through the test hooks `spigot_level_override`, `gearing_level_override`, `delivery_level_override`, `heated_level_override`), and a row's `PreviewTile` and the quick-buy bar's preview show the icon of the level that Buy would give (level + 1); at max level they show the max icon (the final look). An upgrade whose look doesn't change (Plant wildflower patch) keeps the single `<id>.png`. The ring's colour and the widen value are placeholders the designer confirms at the 0.3.3 play-test. The per-level recipe rows are tooling: the main session adds them and Rex runs the recipe after Patricia's spigot and `ReachRing` changes land.
