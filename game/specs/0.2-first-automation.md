## Changes

1. **Upgrade shop holds several rows.** Today `shop_ui.gd` shows one item (Worker Swarm Count). It becomes a list of rows, one per upgrade, each with name, level ("Lv 2 / 5", or "Owned" for one-time buys), and a buy button showing the next cost ($, rounded down) that is disabled when unaffordable, maxed or locked. A locked row shows its gate ("Needs Faster Delivery 1"). Costs follow GDD §3: `base × growth^level`. Rows, in order: Worker Swarm Count, Flight Endurance, Heated Uncapping Fork, Centrifuge Gearing, Spigot Flow (item 12), Faster Delivery, Auto-Sell. All rows show at 720 × 1280 without scrolling (item 14). The look of the new UI (`UI_DESIGN.md`: icons over the shots, wrench icon) is **not** in 0.2; the existing panel is extended.

2. **Flight Endurance** (Tree A): +10% bee speed per level, base $15, 1.15× growth, max level 10. Effect: the hive's fill rate is multiplied by `(1 + flight_speed_bonus_per_level × level)`, on top of Swarm (Terry A1: faster trips fill faster). The visible bees fly faster by the same factor (`bees.gd` `speed`).

3. **Heated Uncapping Fork** (Tree B): base $40, 1.22× growth, max level 5.
   - Fork reach: the player takes the fork from its icon and drags it through the combs; each drag point uncaps every capped cell within `uncap_radius × (1 + heated_fork_radius_bonus_per_level × level)` of the hit cell (level 0 = the hit cell only, today's behaviour). Reach is the Heated Fork's only manual effect in 0.2 (designer, 2026-11-09: "heated fork increases the fork reach"; one continuous sweep is the Long Fork's job).
   - The GDD's "+5% batch bonus for clean cuts" is left out of 0.2: its rule is undefined (listed in the README's Outstanding checklist; nothing to build for it now).
   - After automation (`heated_fork_post_auto_yield_bonus_per_level` 0.04) applies only once the auto-scraper is owned, so it has no effect in 0.2; the key and its use are coded and tested with the Dev toggle "own auto-scraper".  
   - The look (designer, 2026-11-09): the game has no 3D fork yet (`ForkIcon` is a button), so 0.2 adds the uncapping fork as a prop: it appears at the drag point over the combs while the player drags, and leaves when the drag ends. Model: `scenes/variants/fork-r4/fork-r4-c.tscn` (comb fork after the Harvest icon: five round tines, rounded crossbar, ferrule, wooden capsule handle).
   - Heated (Heated Fork ≥ 1): the whole metal head (tines, crossbar, ferrule) eases steel → crank orange → steel once per `fork_pulse_seconds`, as an albedo colour tween with **no emission** (STYLE_GUIDE V2); `fork-r4-c-hot.tscn` is the peak colour. The handle stays wood. At Heated 0 the head stays steel.

4. **Centrifuge Gearing** (Tree B): base $35, 1.20× growth, max level 5. Crank turns needed are multiplied by `(1 − gearing_cut_per_level × level)` (−15%/level, rounded up, at least 1), and the hold-to-crank ramp time likewise. Bottling time is unchanged. The after-automation cut (`gearing_post_auto_time_cut_per_level` 0.08) applies only with the motor owned, so it has no effect in 0.2; coded and tested with the Dev toggle "own motor".
   - The look (designer, 2026-11-09): a gear train where the crank meets the lid. **Gear1 sits under the crank pivot at every level**, the crank arm resting on it (the `Crank` node raised 0.095 over today's centrifuge); each Gearing level adds one gear, so level N shows Gear1..Gear(N + 1): one at 0, six at 5. Steel gears with dark hubs and pointed teeth; Gear2..Gear6 sit on a ring concentric with the lid (following its circumference, near the rim), each meshing with the one before; every gear has its own tooth count (16, 17, 11, 10, 8, 6); turning with the crank (each gear opposite to its neighbour). Model: `scenes/variants/gearing-r6/gearing-r6-b5.tscn` (all six gears; `gearing-r6-b0.tscn` shows level 0), generated by `_tools/gear_train.py`.

5. **Faster Delivery** (Automation, levels): base $60, 1.30× growth, max level 4. The truck's trip time is `truck_trip_seconds × (1 − faster_delivery_trip_cut_per_level × level)` (30 → 25.5 → 21 → 16.5 → 12 s).
   - The look (designer, 2026-11-09): the truck gains hot rod parts level by level, and nothing disappears on an upgrade. **No flames come from the exhaust at any level**; flames are crank orange (STYLE_GUIDE Accent).

     | Faster Delivery | Shows | From the pick |
     |---|---|---|
     | 0 | today's truck | `scenes/props/truck.tscn` |
     | 1 | `Stacks`: two exhaust pipes on the cab | `truck-flames-r3/truck-flames-r3-l1.tscn` |
     | 2 | `Stacks` + `DecalsSmall` (small flame decals on the cab sides) | decals of `truck-flames-r1/truck-flames-r1-a.tscn` |
     | 3 | `Stacks` + `DecalsBold` (bold flame decals) | decals of `truck-flames-r1/truck-flames-r1-b.tscn` |
     | 4 | `Stacks` + `DecalsBold` + `FlamesTop` (flames over the cab roof) | `truck-flames-r3/truck-flames-r3-l4.tscn` |

6. **Auto-Sell** (Automation, one-time $250, unlocks at Faster Delivery level 1): once owned, the truck leaves by itself when its bed is full and sells at the full price, exactly as a Sell press would. Sell stays pressable for a part load. A small "Auto" tag shows by the Sell button while Auto-Sell is owned.

7. **Phase 2 flag.** Buying Auto-Sell (designer, 2026-11-09; Faster Delivery does not trigger it) sets `Economy.phase = 2` (GDD §1). In 0.2 the flag has no gameplay effect; the Dev panel shows it, and a one-line toast "Phase 2: your farm starts running itself" shows once.

8. **Bee Journal entry, EN/FR, through Godot's translation CSV.** One approved entry from `content/bee-journal-real.md`: Entry 3, Bumble Bees (a Sunny Meadow visitor; designer, 2026-11-09: any Meadow species) is exported by a script, `_tools/journal_csv.py`, into `game/translations/journal.csv` (keys `JOURNAL_<ID>_TITLE`, `JOURNAL_<ID>_BODY`; columns `en`, `fr`), imported by Godot and registered in `project.godot`. A **Journal** button in the shop panel opens a panel showing the title and body through `tr()`. The game's locale follows the OS (`en` fallback); the Dev panel has an EN/FR toggle. The text is the approved text verbatim; nothing is retyped by hand.

9. **The second stop only after Spin** (designer's play-test of 0.2.0, 2026-11-09: "camera should only zoom to second zoom screen after spin is tapped"). A full bucket no longer ends the first zoom or pans the camera: the player keeps harvesting (cells go to the pile), and when the zoom ends (all frames done, or Back) the camera returns to the farm view. The camera goes to the second stop only when Spin is tapped (0.1.4 item 11, unchanged). Replaces the 0.1.3 play-test rule "a full bucket pans to the second stop" (`_filled_in_zoom`, `_closing_to_second` in `main.gd`) and its test in `test_camera.gd`.

10. **The fork goes back into the Harvest icon** (same play-test: "fork stays in after harvesting is done/when mouse is done clicking, after harvesting the hive it should go back into the icon"; `UI_DESIGN.md` rule 4, Harvest is the fork icon). The fork prop never stays on screen without a drag: it hides when the press ends, wherever the release lands, and when the frame or zoom ends during a drag. When the zoom ends, a fork still on screen moves to the Harvest icon's spot and shrinks into it over `fork_return_seconds` (config, 0.35, live in Dev), then hides.

11. **The lid stays still; the crank drives the gears** (same play-test: "the lid shouldn't spin the 0 gear spins along with the handle, which makes the other gears spin"). Today `centrifuge.gd` turns the whole `Lid` with `spin_speed`, so the lid disc, crank and gear train all spin as one. Instead: `LidDisc` and the gear ring never rotate; the `Crank` turns about its own pivot (over Gear1); Gear1 turns with the crank at the same rate and direction, and Gear2..Gear6 turn from it as item 4 says (opposite to their neighbour, speed by tooth count). The basket inside (`Spinner`) keeps spinning as today.

12. **Spigot Flow** (Tree B; designer's play-test of 0.2.0, 2026-11-09: "the jar filing rate has become the bottleneck"; moved from after 0.2 into 0.2.1, designer 2026-11-09). A new shop row after Centrifuge Gearing: base $30, 1.20× growth, max level 5 (provisional placeholders, erring slow; Terry checks them before 0.3.0). Bottling time per jar is `bottling_seconds_per_jar × (1 − spigot_flow_cut_per_level × level)` (−12%/level: 3.0 → 1.8 s at level 5); the jar's flight time is unchanged. No model change in 0.2.1.

13. **No jar flies after a leaving truck** (same play-test: "if the jars are filing and the truck is leaving the jar flies towards the truck"). Today a bottled jar is counted on the truck at once and flies to its bed slot, and Sell or Auto-Sell can send the truck off while that jar is still in the air. The truck now leaves only once every jar flying to its bed has landed (Sell and Auto-Sell both wait); a jar bottled after the truck has left flies to the crate, as today.

14. **The shop shows every row without scrolling** (same play-test: "I don't like the scrolling menu, the menus that have been implemented do work"). The shop panel is tall enough to show all seven rows at once at 720 × 1280, with no scroll container, and still clears the HUD. Replaces item 1's "the panel scrolls if the rows do not fit" and the C1 hand fix (`ShopScroll` one-row height). A stopgap: 0.3.0's Shop sheet replaces the panel.

**Config and Dev mode** (Process 3): every value is in `default_config.tres`, live in the Dev panel: `flight_speed_bonus_per_level` (0.10), `flight_base_cost` (15.0), `flight_cost_growth` (1.15), `flight_max_level` (10), `heated_fork_radius_bonus_per_level` (0.12), `fork_pulse_seconds` (1.2), `fork_return_seconds` (0.35), `heated_fork_base_cost` (40.0), `heated_fork_cost_growth` (1.22), `heated_fork_max_level` (5), `heated_fork_post_auto_yield_bonus_per_level` (0.04), `uncap_radius` (Patricia sets it so level 0 uncaps one cell), `gearing_cut_per_level` (0.15), `gearing_base_cost` (35.0), `gearing_cost_growth` (1.20), `gearing_max_level` (5), `gearing_post_auto_time_cut_per_level` (0.08), `gearing_post_auto_cut_includes_bottling` (false), `spigot_flow_base_cost` (30.0), `spigot_flow_cost_growth` (1.20), `spigot_flow_max_level` (5), `spigot_flow_cut_per_level` (0.12), `faster_delivery_base_cost` (60.0), `faster_delivery_cost_growth` (1.30), `faster_delivery_max_level` (4), `faster_delivery_trip_cut_per_level` (0.15), `auto_sell_cost` (250.0), `auto_sell_unlock_delivery_level` (1).
