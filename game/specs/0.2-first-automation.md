## Changes

1. **Upgrade shop holds several rows.** Today `shop_ui.gd` shows one item (Worker Swarm Count). It becomes a list of rows, one per upgrade, each with name, level ("Lv 2 / 5", or "Owned" for one-time buys), and a buy button showing the next cost ($, rounded down) that is disabled when unaffordable, maxed or locked. A locked row shows its gate ("Needs Faster Delivery 1"). Costs follow GDD §3: `base × growth^level`. Rows, in order: Worker Swarm Count, Flight Endurance, Heated Uncapping Fork, Centrifuge Gearing, Faster Delivery, Auto-Sell. The panel scrolls if the rows do not fit at 720 × 1280. The look of the new UI (`UI_DESIGN.md`: icons over the shots, wrench icon) is **not** in 0.2; the existing panel is extended.

2. **Flight Endurance** (Tree A): +10% bee speed per level, base $15, 1.15× growth, max level 10. Effect: the hive's fill rate is multiplied by `(1 + flight_speed_bonus_per_level × level)`, on top of Swarm (Terry A1: faster trips fill faster). The visible bees fly faster by the same factor (`bees.gd` `speed`).

3. **Heated Uncapping Fork** (Tree B): base $40, 1.22× growth, max level 5.
   - Fork reach: the player takes the fork from its icon and drags it through the combs; each drag point uncaps every capped cell within `uncap_radius × (1 + heated_fork_radius_bonus_per_level × level)` of the hit cell (level 0 = the hit cell only, today's behaviour). Reach is the Heated Fork's only manual effect in 0.2 (designer, 2026-11-09: "heated fork increases the fork reach"; one continuous sweep is the Long Fork's job).
   - The GDD's "+5% batch bonus for clean cuts" is left out of 0.2: its rule is undefined (README TBD).
   - After automation (`heated_fork_post_auto_yield_bonus_per_level` 0.04) applies only once the auto-scraper is owned, so it has no effect in 0.2; the key and its use are coded and tested with the Dev toggle "own auto-scraper".  
   - The look (designer, 2026-11-09): the game has no 3D fork yet (`ForkIcon` is a button), so 0.2 adds the uncapping fork as a prop: it appears at the drag point over the combs while the player drags, and leaves when the drag ends. Model: `scenes/variants/fork-r4/fork-r4-c.tscn` (comb fork after the Harvest icon: five round tines, rounded crossbar, ferrule, wooden capsule handle).
   - Heated (Heated Fork ≥ 1): the whole metal head (tines, crossbar, ferrule) eases steel → crank orange → steel once per `fork_pulse_seconds`, as an albedo colour tween with **no emission** (STYLE_GUIDE V2); `fork-r4-c-hot.tscn` is the peak colour. The handle stays wood. At Heated 0 the head stays steel.

4. **Centrifuge Gearing** (Tree B): base $35, 1.20× growth, max level 5. Crank turns needed are multiplied by `(1 − gearing_cut_per_level × level)` (−15%/level, rounded up, at least 1), and the hold-to-crank ramp time likewise. Bottling time is unchanged. The after-automation cut (`gearing_post_auto_time_cut_per_level` 0.08) applies only with the motor owned, so it has no effect in 0.2; coded and tested with the Dev toggle "own motor".
   - The look (designer, 2026-11-09): a gear train where the crank meets the lid. **Gear1 sits under the crank pivot at every level**, the crank arm resting on it (the `Crank` node raised 0.095 over today's centrifuge); each Gearing level adds one gear, so level N shows Gear1..Gear(N + 1): one at 0, six at 5. Notched steel gears with dark hubs; Gear2..Gear6 sit on a ring concentric with the lid (following its circumference), each meshing with the one before; every gear has its own tooth count; turning with the crank (each gear opposite to its neighbour). Model: `scenes/variants/gearing-r5/gearing-r5-a5.tscn` (all six gears; `gearing-r5-a0.tscn` shows level 0), generated by `_tools/gear_train.py`. Ring size is the round-5 vote (a5 = tight ring, b5 = nearer the rim); the spec names the pick.

5. **Faster Delivery** (Automation, levels): base $60, 1.30× growth, max level 4. The truck's trip time is `truck_trip_seconds × (1 − faster_delivery_trip_cut_per_level × level)` (30 → 25.5 → 21 → 16.5 → 12 s).
   - The look (designer, 2026-11-09): the truck gains hot rod parts level by level, and nothing disappears on an upgrade. **No flames come from the exhaust at any level**; flames are crank orange (STYLE_GUIDE Accent).

     | Faster Delivery | Shows | From the pick |
     |---|---|---|
     | 0 | today's truck | `scenes/props/truck.tscn` |
     | 1 | `Stacks`: two exhaust pipes on the cab | `truck-flames-r3/truck-flames-r3-l1.tscn` |
     | 2 | `Stacks` + `DecalsSmall` (small flame decals on the cab sides) | decals of `truck-flames-r1/truck-flames-r1-a.tscn` |
     | 3 | `Stacks` + `DecalsBold` (bold flame decals) | decals of `truck-flames-r1/truck-flames-r1-b.tscn` |
     | 4 | `Stacks` + `DecalsBold` + `FlamesTop` (flames over the cab roof) | `truck-flames-r3/truck-flames-r3-l4.tscn` |

6. **Auto-Sell** (Automation, one-time $250, unlocks at Faster Delivery level 1): once owned, the truck leaves by itself when its bed is full and sells at the full price, exactly as a Sell press would. Sell stays pressable for a part load. A small "Auto" tag shows by the Sell button while Auto-Sell is owned.

7. **Phase 2 flag.** Buying Auto-Sell (designer, 2026-11-09; Faster Delivery does not trigger it) sets `Economy.phase = 2` (GDD §1). In 0.2 the flag has no gameplay effect; the Dev panel shows it, and a one-line toast "Phase 2: your farm starts running itself" shows once.

8. **Bee Journal entry, EN/FR, through Godot's translation CSV.** One approved entry from `content/bee-journal-real.md`: Entry 3, Bumble Bees (a Sunny Meadow visitor; designer, 2026-11-09: any Meadow species) is exported by a script, `_tools/journal_csv.py`, into `game/translations/journal.csv` (keys `JOURNAL_<ID>_TITLE`, `JOURNAL_<ID>_BODY`; columns `en`, `fr`), imported by Godot and registered in `project.godot`. A **Journal** button in the shop panel opens a panel showing the title and body through `tr()`. The game's locale follows the OS (`en` fallback); the Dev panel has an EN/FR toggle. The text is the approved text verbatim; nothing is retyped by hand.

**Config and Dev mode** (Process 3): every value is in `default_config.tres`, live in the Dev panel: `flight_speed_bonus_per_level` (0.10), `flight_base_cost` (15.0), `flight_cost_growth` (1.15), `flight_max_level` (10), `heated_fork_radius_bonus_per_level` (0.12), `fork_pulse_seconds` (1.2), `heated_fork_base_cost` (40.0), `heated_fork_cost_growth` (1.22), `heated_fork_max_level` (5), `heated_fork_post_auto_yield_bonus_per_level` (0.04), `uncap_radius` (Patricia sets it so level 0 uncaps one cell), `gearing_cut_per_level` (0.15), `gearing_base_cost` (35.0), `gearing_cost_growth` (1.20), `gearing_max_level` (5), `gearing_post_auto_time_cut_per_level` (0.08), `gearing_post_auto_cut_includes_bottling` (false), `faster_delivery_base_cost` (60.0), `faster_delivery_cost_growth` (1.30), `faster_delivery_max_level` (4), `faster_delivery_trip_cut_per_level` (0.15), `auto_sell_cost` (250.0), `auto_sell_unlock_delivery_level` (1). Dev toggles: own motor, own auto-scraper (test hooks for the post-automation bonuses), Phase, EN/FR, "+$100" money. Placeholders err slow.

1. **Models session: done 2026-11-09.** Four vote rounds on Design Votes (Milo for rounds 1–3, `_tools/compose_variant.py` after); each round's votes and notes in `game/reports/variants/<prop>-r<N>/decisions.json`. 2. **Implementation session:** the spec is approved, and `/goal` + `goal_agent.py` builds it. Each picked prop gets a pick match (`_tools/pick_match.py`, `game/CONTEXT.md` Process 6): the fork against `fork-r4-c` (cold) and `-c-hot` (Heated at the pulse peak), the centrifuge at Gearing 0 against `gearing-r5-a0` and at 5 against the six-gear pick, the truck at levels 1 and 4 against `truck-flames-r3-l1` / `-l4` and at 2 and 3 against `truck-flames-r1-a` / `-b` with the stacks added.

No other 0.2 upgrade changes a model (Flight Endurance and Auto-Sell are numbers, the shop row and the Auto tag).
