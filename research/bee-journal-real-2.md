## Not supported

- "they are among Canada's most jewel-bright native pollinators" (Entry 1, sweat bees): No Canadian government, NGO, or academic source found that ranks sweat bees as the most jewel-bright or most brilliantly coloured among Canadian native pollinators. The WWF Canada page (https://wwf.ca/stories/world-bee-day-native-pollinators/) describes *Agapostemon virescens* as "metallic green towards its front end and boldly striped on its back half" but makes no comparative ranking. A targeted web search ("most colourful" / "most jewel-bright" / "most brilliantly coloured" native pollinators Canada) returned no source using these phrases as a ranking for sweat bees across Canada. The comparison clause has no source support. The prior brief's Claim 1 supports only that some sweat bee species display metallic green colouring; the "among Canada's most" framing goes further than any found quote.
