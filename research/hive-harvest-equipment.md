## GDD issues
- GDD says "a frame worth 10 jars" (growing to 25 with Deep Frame) and the free buffer "holds one extra full frame (10 jars at base...)". Sources: a full capped deep frame is about 6 lb (R5), a medium about 3.5 lb (R6). Jar size is not sourced here, so whether 10 jars is plausible depends on the designer's jar weight (about 0.6 kg per jar would be a rough match, unsourced). Flag only; not a contradiction without a jar size.
- GDD says uncapping with a fork: "dragging across the matte yellow capped wax cells scrapes them off". Source (R11): the hot knife is the main tool and the fork/scratcher is a clean-up pass; cappings come off as wax slices (R12), not as individual cells.
- GDD says "Each scraped cell pops and flies into the empty centrifuge." Source (R10, R13): whole uncapped frames are placed in the extractor; the cappings are collected separately as wax and drained (R13). No source has cappings entering the extractor.
- GDD says honey sprays "against its glass walls". No source fetched describes extractor walls; Mann Lake's search snippet describes stainless steel (not fetched). Glass walls are unsourced.
- GDD says the centrifuge has "a physical crank on its cylinder". Where the crank mounts (lid vs crossbar) is not sourced.
- GDD says the centrifuge "holds several frames (capacity TBD)". R14 gives one data point (2 deep, 2 medium or 4 shallow), consistent.
- GDD says Frames "come from the hive". Consistent with R3/R4 (frames are movable and hang in the box).
