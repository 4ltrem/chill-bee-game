- Lore: I compared the draft against GDD §2 (harvest), §3 (Trees A and B), §7, §8 and §9. It only sets up the hand-off to free play and does not push a purchase. Beat 7 asks for an in-game upgrade only. No donations or real-money purchases are mentioned.

  - "Every flower they visit fills the hive a little more" and "Your bees are already out there foraging". These are in-world mechanics and are acceptable.
  - French matches the English in meaning, with one exception: Beat 7 "more honey for us!" is rendered "plus de miel pour tout le monde!".
  - No fictional species appear.
