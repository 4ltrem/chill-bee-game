Royal Jelly icon: pick one

Every variant puts the number first and the icon after it, with the crown upright. In each pill the 12, the icon and the + are separate elements, so each can be selected and moved. Large = 120 px preview; pill = the real 1× size on the phone boards.

12+
Closest to now: the dipper keeps its angle, the crown sits level on the bowl's tip.
B · Upright dipper, crown on top
12+
A crowned dipper standing straight: symmetrical, reads like a little royal figure.
C · Diagonal dipper with a drip
12+
A plus a honey drop beside it: says "jelly" as well as "royal".
D · Crowned jelly pot
12+
No dipper: a small pot with the crown as its lid knob. Reads clearly at 1×.
E · Crown over a drop
12+
Simplest: crown and drop, the boldest shapes at small size. Close to the nectar drop icon, though.
F · Dipper flat, crown on the handle end
12+
Dipper lying level, dripping; the crown sits upright on the handle end.
G · Crowned honeycomb cell
12+
One flat-topped cell with the crown resting on its top edge. Boldest of the comb set.
H · Three-cell comb, crowned
12+
A little comb of three cells, the crown on the top cell. Matches the hive's hex cells.
I · Crown with honeycomb jewels
12+
The crown is the icon; its jewels are three honeycomb cells. Can't be mistaken for the nectar drop.
J · Wax cell with a jelly drop
12+
A pale wax cell holding a golden drop, crowned: a queen cell full of royal jelly.
