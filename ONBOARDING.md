### Skills to Know About
- /end-of-day — closes the working day: exports the session, logs decisions, rebuilds the decision page, runs the checks and commits. Run it when you say "end of work day".
- /goal — sets a goal that Claude works toward across turns; used to drive milestone builds.
- /context and /compact — check and shrink the context window; long sessions here are common.
- /btw — ask a side question without derailing the main session.
- /advisor — get a second opinion from a stronger reviewer model before big changes.
