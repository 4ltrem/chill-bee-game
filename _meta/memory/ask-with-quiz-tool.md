Whenever I need more answers from the designer, I ask them through the AskUserQuestion tool (the "quiz tool"): up to 4 questions per call, 2–4 options each, recommended option first with "(Recommended)".

**Why:** designer, 2026-11-09: "whenever you need more answer ask me the questions using the quiz tool", after I listed four open questions on Build mode as text.

**Give every option a `preview`** (a short ASCII mock-up or flow): with previews the designer can tab and add notes to a choice; without them they can't, and they rejected a preview-less quiz for that (2026-11-10). Read the notes as part of the answer.

**How to apply:** don't end a reply with a list of questions; put them in AskUserQuestion. Each question must stand on its own (the dialog may hide earlier text). More than 4 questions → several calls. Related: [[session-handoff]].
